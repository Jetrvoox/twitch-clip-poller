"Darwin Project Clipping" ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial project that discovers publicly available Twitch clips of the game Darwin Project, allows a single human operator to review and approve individual clips, and — for approved clips only — posts a reformatted version to TikTok.

What this app does

  • Polls the public Twitch API for clips of a specific game.
  • Presents candidate clips to the operator for manual approval or rejection.
  • Only clips explicitly approved by the operator are reformatted and posted.
  • No content is posted automatically without human review.
